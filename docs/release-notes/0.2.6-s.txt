Legion 0.2.6-s: agents can now work on your computer, under your rules.

- Agent shell: an agent you allow can run commands and edit files on your computer, inside a folder you choose. Every action goes through its approval mode and your Gates, and a hard guard refuses whole-machine damage for every agent, full access included. No key Legion holds reaches the model, and .env files are masked by default.
- Gates: rules in plain sentences decide where approvals apply. On an approval card, Remember turns your answer into a rule in one click. Block rules hold even in Full access. After you allow the same kind of call 3 times, Gates suggests the rule; you can switch suggestions off.
- The Marshal works the board: it picks the next item, matches it to the right bot, sends work out in waves, watches for stalls and gives each bot at most two tries.
- One task list per conversation, on Claude and on other providers, kept through compaction and through switching between them.
- Models without tool calling can now use tools: Legion gives them the tools in the prompt, with the same approvals.
- Fixed: changing a provider, project members, the CI repository or the board leader was refused by the app and now saves again; agent-to-agent threads showed several times their real cost; the model you chose for an agent now always wins; long replies are kept whole.

Known limit: the agent shell has no sandbox yet; it runs commands as you. Keep it off for agents on Full access unless you trust their work.

Installs over 0.2.5-o and later. Full detail in CHANGELOG.md.
