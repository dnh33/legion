This update is a larger download than usual, about 285 MB, because it changes the parts Legion is built on. It installs by itself.

Keep your knowledge fresh
The Lattice's Lint tab lists notes that may be out of date, with their age and sources. Refresh asks the Archivist to re-check a note and propose an update you accept in the Inbox. Still current marks a note you checked yourself.

Also new:
- The model picker shows every model. Older ones sit under Older models, and OpenRouter models are grouped by their maker.
- No focus rings. The keyboard shows a soft tint instead. Settings, Accessibility brings rings back.
Fixed:
- "Needs you" opens the agent's chat with the item on top.
- Work items on the Board open fully in view, and the Lattice no longer goes blank around a note with many links.

Legion now needs Node 22.12 or newer if you run it from source. Full detail in CHANGELOG.md.
